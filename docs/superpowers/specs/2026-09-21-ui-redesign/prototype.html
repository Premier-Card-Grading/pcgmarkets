    Archivo, one variable family. Wide and heavy for prices, like the HP numerals on a card. Condensed and heavy for card numbers, like the number printed in the card's corner. Normal width for everything else.

    • Reference object is the card shop, not the auction house: price stickers on sleeves, grade badges that read like a stat.
    • The grade badge is the signature element. Gold badge means PCG 10, ink badge means everything else. Same badge everywhere: strip, results, guide, sales, review.
    • Header is an ink band with a gold search button, so the app has a face.
    • Corners 8 to 14px, real shadows only under things that are "objects": cards, stickers, the search field, the guide-price box.

    Home

    • "Just sold" is a horizontal strip of card art with the price on a tilted sticker and the grade badge on the corner. It scrolls and snaps on phones.
    • The headline is heavy and wide with one gold word, then the search.
    • Browse by set is a compact two-column grid with sale counts.

    Search results

    • The query is the headline; the count sits beside it.
    • Each result shows its number as a condensed ink chip, and three grade prices on the right: gold 10 first and largest, 9.5 and 9 smaller. Phones show only the 10.
    • Rows lift a pixel on hover. Language filter is chips.
    • Needs the search API to return 9.5 and 9 medians too.

    Card

    • Pick a grade, see one card. The grade is a select, shadcn style: a trigger showing the current badge, grade and sale count. On desktop it opens a popover list; on phones it opens a bottom drawer. Defaults to PCG 10 Gem Mint. Try it.
    • The guide-price card shows that grade only: price in wide heavy type, change versus the previous window, sale count, range, last sale, sparkline.
    • Sales and the chart follow the selection. Only that grade's sales are listed, and the chart plots only those points with its median line. Try clicking the grades.
    • Masthead: name heavy, English name and rarity beneath, then the number as a condensed ink chip with set, language and release date as chips.
    • Card art sits large with a real shadow, no frame, no caption. Sticky on desktop; centred at about 250px wide on phones.
    • Sales: badge, native price heavy, USD lighter beside it. Chart keeps the gold median line.

    Admin sign-in

    • One white form with a shadow, a heavy heading, and a sent state that tells you what happens next.

    Review queue

    • Reasons are chips with gold counts on the active one.
    • Listing on the left, resolution on the right. Extracted fields as labelled values, missing ones in grey.
    • The active item is outlined in teal. Assign is disabled until a card is picked.

    Health

    • One tile per job in two columns, failed and suspect first with a coloured outline, and the result in plain words.
    • Listings and connectors as ruled key-value tables; runs table in a scrollable box on phones.
